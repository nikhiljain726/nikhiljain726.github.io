Why I keep this site

This is a notebook I happen to keep in public. I have always thought better on the page, so I write up the systems I build and the calls I get wrong, first for myself, so I stop relearning the same lesson, and then for anyone doing similar work.

Making it public changes how I write. A note I might keep sloppy in a private doc has to actually hold up when a stranger can read it and disagree. That pressure is the point. If something here saves you time, or gives you a reason to message me and tell me I am wrong, both are wins.
