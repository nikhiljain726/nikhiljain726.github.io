Growth and performance marketing

For D2C and consumer brands, and early-stage teams that need a small marketing budget to work harder than it should. Acquire where intent is cheapest, run the relationship where the customer already is, and automate the boring middle so a handful of people can hold a large operation.

What I do
  1. Performance marketing at scale. Meta, Google, and programmatic, structured to isolate what works and pour into it. I have managed over 50 crore in annual ad spend across top e-commerce brands.
  2. D2C growth, end to end. I built and ran my own brand to 3 crore in year one and 14,000-plus customers, mostly through Meta and Google, so I know the operation, not just the ad account.
  3. Lifecycle and retention. CRM and CDP journeys in Clevertap, MoEngage, and WebEngage, WhatsApp commerce flows, and email treated as a real channel rather than an afterthought.
  4. Analytics and attribution. Get the data honest first, reconcile what the platforms disagree on, then decide what matters. I have built internal tools to shorten the gap between a signal and a decision.
  5. Creative strategy. On the platforms as they are now, the creative is the targeting. I treat it that way.
  6. Owned-channel growth. Cut paid dependence with organic outbound and a re-engineered email motion. At one company that doubled qualified leads and grew email to about 30 percent of the mix.
