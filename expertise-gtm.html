GTM engineering and growth for B2B

For B2B SaaS, marketplaces, and retail media teams that want their pipeline to run like a system, not a set of manual habits. Most of my work is one shape: take the signals already lying around, turn them into something a rep can act on the same day, and wire it so it keeps running without anyone remembering to.

What I do
  1. CRM infrastructure and cleanup. Dedupe and merge at scale, ICP fields that force a decision on every record, and a CRM an agent can actually read and write, not a read-only connector. I built and open-sourced a 53-tool Pipedrive MCP for exactly this.
  2. Signal-to-action pipelines. LinkedIn engagement, website intent, and competitive intel turned into a short, ranked, same-day list a rep can work, not a dashboard they argue with.
  3. Enrichment, scoring, and routing. Daily workflows that keep the base clean, enrich new records, and re-score against ICP as the definition of a good account shifts.
  4. Next-best-action agents. Turn a forty-deal pipeline review into the three that need a human, each written into the CRM with a reason attached.
  5. Outbound GTM. Sequences tuned for reply quality over volume, wired to the CRM so nothing falls through.
  6. Recurring automation. Set up with Claude Code and tools like nRev so the work runs on a schedule, not on your memory.
