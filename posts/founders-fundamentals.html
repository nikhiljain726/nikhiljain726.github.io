The correction is an opening for founders with real economics

For a decade the market paid storytellers. The correction hands the advantage back to businesses that actually work.

D2C & founder lessons  ·  Mar 2023  ·  4 min read

For most of the last decade, the market rewarded storytellers. Founders who could sell a narrative raised money whether or not the business underneath it worked. That is changing. The bubble has burst, and the correction running through the market has moved the goal from growth at all costs to profitability and EBITDA. The fundamentals are back in charge.

Kunal Shah is the example people reach for. He built his name raising money for Freecharge and selling it to Snapdeal, reportedly for around 400 million dollars. Snapdeal was under real financial strain at the time, and later sold Freecharge to Axis Bank for a reported 60 million. That first outcome still helped him raise for Cred, now one of the highest-valued companies in the ecosystem, though Cred has carried heavy losses of its own, with reported losses of roughly 1,280 crore against revenue of around 400 crore in FY22. I am not using this to knock his ability to build, he clearly can. The point is narrower. A story opens the door. A business has to create value for everyone in the room, customers, investors, partners, and founders, to stay open.

The correction is a real opening for founders with strong unit economics. There is less competition for capital and attention now, and a business that works has an edge it did not have when everything got funded. The companies that create a genuine win for customers, investors, partners, and founders at the same time are the ones that last.

It is a harder market for anyone who was raising on a story alone. Investors want financial stability, and a good deck no longer stands in for a path to profit. Money will be more selective from here, underwriting the numbers and the durability of the model rather than the pitch.

If you are running a business with real economics and real traction, this is your window. The correction is not the threat. For founders who actually build, it is the opening.
