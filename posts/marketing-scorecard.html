The marketing scorecard I plan the year around

GTM & growth engineering  ·  2023  ·  3 min read

In marketing, what gets measured gets managed. That is why I plan every year around a scorecard: one view that tracks content engagement, launch readiness, buyer conversations, competitive programs, and revenue influence.

It is not about having data. It is about having the right data.

A few things the scorecard forces:

  • It quantifies how ready your go-to-market actually is, in percentages, not vibes.
  • It counts the conversations that open doors to deeper customer insight.
  • It tracks the competitive programs that sharpen your market edge.
  • And it ties back to revenue, which is the real measure of whether the marketing worked.

The scorecard is the guide for each quarter. It keeps initiatives targeted instead of merely busy, and it makes the quarterly review a conversation about numbers rather than opinions.

Life is simple: plan, act, reflect. The scorecard is just how I keep score while doing it. I keep a simple template for this, so if you want a starting point, email me and I will send it.
