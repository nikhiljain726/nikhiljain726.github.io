Marketing is simple: who, what, where

The framework fits on a napkin. Being honest about the answers is the hard part.

Performance marketing  ·  Aug 2022  ·  2 min read

Marketing is simple, at least the questions are. Three of them decide whether a campaign works.

  1. Who? Your target audience.
  2. What? The thing you are offering them.
  3. Where? Where do you want them to see it?

Answer those honestly and you have a campaign. Get any one of them wrong and no amount of budget saves it. You will spend into the void, wonder why the numbers are soft, and blame the channel.

The framework is not the hard part. Being honest about the answers is.

Most campaigns that miss did not fail on execution. They failed because the who was fuzzy, the what was not compelling, or the where did not match how the audience actually spends attention. Fix those three first, then worry about the creative and the bid.
