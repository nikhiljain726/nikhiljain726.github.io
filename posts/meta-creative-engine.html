Our Meta structure: 80 percent winners, 20 percent chaos

Performance marketing  ·  2025  ·  3 min read

When it comes to Meta campaigns, this is what our structure looks like.

80 percent of budget goes to winning creatives. 20 percent goes to testing new ones. That is the whole engine.

There was a time we would slice and dice audiences like a data chef. Lookalikes, interest stacks, exclusions, the whole targeting circus. But Meta has evolved. The algorithm does the heavy lifting now. What moves the needle is creatives.

It is no longer who you target. It is what you show them.

We run one main campaign with proven winners, and a smaller one purely for testing: hooks, formats, angles, UGC, statics, carousels, chaos. Whatever gives us signal.

The moment a creative hits, it moves up. The rest get killed in 2 to 3 days. No sentiments, no guesswork, only data.

So if your Meta performance is stuck, do not blame the targeting.

Ab creative nahi chala, toh kuch nahi chala.
