Building a stellar customer experience for a D2C brand

A journey teardown I wrote for Mojocare: how a consumer-health brand earns repeats and trust, mapped from first click to post-delivery.

D2C & founder lessons  ·  Sep 2022  ·  3 min read

Repeat users beat new users, and getting there is not luck. It comes from listening to customers, teaching them about their own problem, and keeping them updated before they have to ask. I wrote up how a D2C brand like Mojocare, a digital-first personal-healthcare brand, could build that experience end to end.

The plan maps the journey in three phases: before the first order, where the job is awareness, discovery, and consideration; after the order, where it is nurturing, order updates, and building trust; and after delivery, where feedback, cross-sell, loyalty, and reviews take over. It also covers how to actually implement it, the considerations that matter, and the tools that make it run. The specifics are written for Mojocare, but the shape works for almost any consumer-facing business with a little refinement.
