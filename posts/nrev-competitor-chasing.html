Finding your competitors and who they're chasing

AI & agent workflows  ·  May 2026  ·  3 min read

You can learn a lot about a market by watching who your competitors are chasing.

The workflow starts with finding the competitors themselves, not the obvious three everyone names, but the long tail that keeps showing up in the same deals. From there, the useful signal is engagement: who is interacting with their posts, who their team is connecting with, which accounts keep appearing around them.

Run that through nRev and you get a picture a battlecard never gives you. Not what a competitor says about itself, but which accounts it is spending attention on, and therefore which accounts are in-market right now.

A competitor's attention is a map of the market's intent. Read it.

I keep the output boring on purpose: a list of accounts, the competitor showing interest, and why the row exists, in a schema that drops straight into the CRM. The value is not the intel itself, it is that the intel becomes a target list a rep can work, with a reason to reach out more specific than "we do this too."

The same method points inward. Watching who engages with your own content, filtered to ICP, is the cheapest intent signal you have. Most teams collect it and let it rot. The workflow just makes sure someone acts on it before it goes cold.
