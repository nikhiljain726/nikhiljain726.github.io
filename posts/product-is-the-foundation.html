In D2C, the product is the foundation

D2C & founder lessons  ·  2022  ·  2 min read

In D2C, the product is not just important. It is the foundation. Everything else is built on top of it.

You can have the best tech stack. You can have top-tier performance marketing. You can nail logistics, branding, and packaging. But if the customer does not love what they get in their hands, it is over. No growth hack covers a mediocre product experience, at least not for long.

No growth hack covers a mediocre product experience. Not for long.

That is why, in any D2C play, the first thing to fix is the product. Not because everything else does not matter. It does. It just comes after.

Start with something real. Something that earns repeat purchases without push notifications. The rest follows.
