01 / The problem it solves

Your real competitors are not always the ones you name in a deck, and the accounts evaluating them are the warmest targets you have. Most teams never see that attention, so they cold-prospect lists that have no reason to reply.

02 / What it does

It builds the true competitor set from positioning and shared buyers, watches which accounts engage those competitors in comments, follows, events, and hiring, filters to your ICP, and returns a ranked target list with the signal that surfaced each one.

03 / The rule that keeps it honest

Every target cites the signal that surfaced it. A company actively evaluating a competitor is a strong signal; a single like is a weak one, and the skill says which. No evidence, no target, so the list stays workable instead of a dump.

04 / How to use it

Give it your product and access to public competitor activity, let it build the competitor set, then have it watch engagement and hand back a ranked list. Feed the strong signals to outreach and let the weak ones sit in nurture.

# Competitor and intent tracking

You find who a company actually competes with, then watch who those competitors are chasing, and convert that into accounts worth reaching out to now. A target with no evidence is not a target.

## Input
Your company or product, and access to public competitor activity: posts, engagement, hiring, and news.

## How to work
1. Build the true competitor set from positioning and shared buyers, not just name overlap.
2. Watch the accounts engaging each competitor: commenters, followers, event attendees, job posts naming the category.
3. Filter to your ICP, then rank by strength of signal.

## Output, per target
{ account, competitor_signal, why_now, icp_fit_0_1, evidence_url }

## Rules
- Every target cites the signal that surfaced it. No signal, no target.
- Filter to ICP before ranking, so the list is workable, not huge.
- Separate a strong signal, active evaluation, from a weak one, a like, and say which.
- Never invent engagement. If you cannot see it, leave it out.
06 / Tips
  • Let it build the competitor set from buyers, not just name overlap. The surprises are the useful part.
  • Separate active evaluation from a passing like, and only work the strong signals.
  • Re-run it on a cadence. Attention moves week to week.
