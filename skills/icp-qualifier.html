ICP qualifier and scorer

Claude / agent skill  ·  Use to sort and score accounts against your ICP
01 / The problem it solves

Most pipelines are full of accounts that were never going to close, and reps burn weeks finding that out one call at a time. The fix is to score fit before anyone dials, and to be honest about it. The hard part is not the scoring, it is refusing to reward an account that has a fatal disqualifier just because it looks impressive on paper.

02 / What it does

Give it your ICP definition and a record, or a whole pasted list. For each one it returns a bucket (Customer, Prospect, Partner, or Disqualify), a 0-100 score, the single reason it scored that way, and the biggest risk. It runs on one domain or a batch, and the output drops straight into Clay, Apollo, or a sheet without rework.

03 / Why disqualifiers come first

It checks hard disqualifiers before anything else. If one fires, the account is Disqualify and the scoring stops, and the skill tells you which disqualifier fired. That order is deliberate. A 90 on an account you can never sell to is a more expensive mistake than a 40 on one you can, because the 90 pulls a rep's time toward a dead end.

A high score with a fatal disqualifier is worse than an honest low score.

# ICP qualifier

You score how well a company or contact fits an ICP, so a rep knows who to touch first and why. Be blunt about disqualifiers. A high score with a fatal disqualifier is worse than an honest low score.

## Input
An ICP definition, and one record or a list of records.

## How to work
1. Check the hard disqualifiers first. If any is present, bucket as Disqualify and stop.
2. Otherwise bucket as Customer, Prospect, or Partner.
3. Score 0-100 on the traits that actually predict a win, not the wishlist.

## Output, per record
{ bucket, score_0_100, reason, biggest_risk }

## Rules
- Disqualifiers override the score. Say which one fired.
- The reason is one line, concrete, and specific to the record.
- No fabrication. If a needed fact is missing, say what you would need to confirm the score.
- Keep buckets to the four exact words so the output filters cleanly.
06 / Tips
  • Push it to name the disqualifiers explicitly. That list is more useful than the wishlist.
  • Feed it ten past wins and ten losses first, so its idea of fit is grounded in your real outcomes.
  • Re-run quarterly. Your ICP drifts as the product and market move.
