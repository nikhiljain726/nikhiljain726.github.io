01 / The problem it solves

A company visits your site, reads pricing twice, and leaves. Without a way to resolve and act on that visit it is invisible, and by the time anyone notices, the interest has cooled. Warm traffic goes cold within a day.

02 / What it does

It takes a resolved company visit from a de-anonymization tool, checks it against your ICP, scores the intent from what the visitor actually did, and routes the warm ones to the owning rep the same day, with the pages viewed and a one-line why-now.

03 / The rule that keeps it sharp

Cold or off-ICP traffic is never routed. A perfect-fit company that read one blog post is not intent. Scoring runs on behavior, so pricing and product pages and repeat visits outrank a single read, and reps only get accounts worth a call.

04 / How to use it

Point it at your de-anonymization feed and your CRM, give it your ICP and which pages signal buying intent, and run it through the day. Warm accounts route to a rep; everything else goes to nurture, not to a person.

# Visitor to intent

You take a company that visited the site, decide whether it is worth a rep's time today, and if so route it with a reason. Speed matters: a warm visit cools within a day.

## Input
A resolved company from a de-anonymization tool, the pages viewed, visit depth and recency, and your ICP.

## How to work
1. Check ICP fit first. If it fails, stop and do not route.
2. Score intent from behavior: pricing and product pages and repeat visits outrank a single blog read.
3. Route the warm ones to the owning rep with the account, the pages, and a one-line why-now.

## Output, per visit
{ account, icp_fit, intent_score_0_100, pages, why_now, route_to }

## Rules
- Cold or off-ICP traffic is never routed. Silence is a valid output.
- Score on behavior, not just firmographics. A perfect-fit company reading one blog is not intent.
- Route same day or not at all. Stale visits go to nurture, not to a rep.
- Never fabricate pages or visit data.
06 / Tips
  • Weight pricing, product, and repeat visits far above a blog read.
  • Set a hard ICP gate so reps never get off-target accounts.
  • Route same day or send to nurture. A two-day-old visit is not worth a rep's time.
